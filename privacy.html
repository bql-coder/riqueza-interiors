RIID ARCHITECTURE LIMITED (trading as Riqueza Design, Xcel by Riqueza, and The Pod by Riqueza) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information
When you fill out a contact form, course registration, or booking inquiry, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone / WhatsApp number
  • Project location
  • Course or package preferences
  • Payment receipts and transaction details
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries and provide design consultation

  • To communicate about your projects, orders, or sessions

  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Payment Processing

Payments for academy courses and podcast bookings are processed via bank transfer to our business account with Wema Bank (Account Name: Riqueza Consultar). We store payment confirmation receipts for record-keeping. We do not store credit card details on our servers.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We use FormSubmit to handle form submissions via email. Your form data is transmitted to us by email. We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, or as required by law. Once no longer needed, we will securely delete or anonymise your data.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at designbyriqueza@gmail.com.

Our website does not use cookies for tracking purposes. We may use essential cookies required for the basic functioning of the site. No personal data is collected through cookies.

9. Security
